# Portal header alignment — implementation evidence

Date: 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-25. Baseline: `9270cdd` (`feat/appearance-preference`, PR #96) plus working tree on local branch `feat/portal-header-alignment`. Scope: [correction handoff](portal-header-alignment-handoff.md). Committed on `feat/portal-header-alignment`; not deployed; no staging or live data touched.

## Delivered

- **Search slot:** `PortalShell` has an optional `search` slot between the masthead and `.wd-portal-actions`, rendered only when supplied. Partner lead search moved from `#actions` to `#search` in `PartnerChrome` (fixture included); page actions such as Brand's Invite partners stay in `#actions`. No search was added to Brand or Professional.
- **Search style:** shared `.wd-portal-search` styles in `portal-shell.css`: `flex: 1 1 320px`, `max-width: 720px`, 44px, 10px radius, `rgb(255 255 255 / 14%)` surface, transparent input with inherited white text and icon, 80% white placeholder, mint focus ring. Brand's light header gets a paper surface with a line border, ink text and muted placeholder and icon instead. Below 768px the slot takes its own full-width row.
- **Account button:** `AccountMenu` accepts an optional `workspaceLabel`; with it, the trigger becomes one outlined button (white 35% border, or the control line on Brand) with the label, then the existing 34px initials avatar, per v2. Accessible name: “Account menu: ‹workspace› ‹initials›”; long names truncate with an ellipsis and a `title`. It opens the same account menu (identity, workspace switch, privacy, appearance, sign-out). Without a label, for example on account and workspace selection pages, the avatar-only button is unchanged.
- **Workspace name placement:** `PortalShell` passes its existing `workspaceName` (selected workspace from shared props, or the fixture override) to the account button and no longer repeats it beside the logo; the experience chip stays. The drawer description keeps the name. With no selected workspace the label is omitted.
- **Layout:** the masthead (logo and experience) no longer shrinks, so the account label truncates instead of overlapping it. From 768px there is one row: masthead, then search, then actions on the right, with 16px gaps. At 600–767px search takes its own row below. Under 600px every experience uses two rows, forced by an explicit flex line break rather than exact-fit widths: Menu and branding, then search (Partner only) and actions/account sharing the width in desktop order. The account button is a little tighter there (10px left padding, 32px avatar, 44px minimum height) and never narrower than its avatar and padding.

- **Navigation scrollbar (follow-up, 2026-11-25):** the user asked whether the always-visible sidebar scrollbar should hide. **Confirmed:** the recommended treatment was approved. The sidebar list and drawer list (`.wd-portal-scroll`) use a thin scrollbar that is transparent at rest and shown in the control-line colour on hover or when focus is inside, with a stable gutter so nothing shifts. Where scroll-driven animations are supported (Chromium), a 28px mask fades the list only at an edge with more items beyond it; elsewhere it simply scrolls. `scroll-padding-block` keeps focused items clear of the fades. The drawer now scrolls only its navigation below a fixed title and Close row. Measured in Chrome at 1440×640: transparent at rest, `rgb(217, 203, 179)` on hover and keyboard focus, fades 0/28px at the top, 28/28px mid-list and 28/0px at the bottom; no fade or overflow at 1440×1400; collapsed sidebar and the 390×600 drawer behave the same, with Close visible at both ends. After this change: Vitest 252/252, format, typecheck and build pass; `portal-header`, `portal-home`, `partner-navigation`, `professional-ui`, `brand-lead-visibility` and `accessibility` 28/28 on Chromium desktop + mobile.
- **Collapse below the fold (fixed, 2026-11-25, user request):** the sticky sidebar was sized to the full window height but started below the header, so on short windows Collapse sat under the fold until the page scrolled. `.wd-portal-sidebar-inner` is now `calc(100dvh - --wd-portal-header-height)` (64px, 65px on Brand's bordered header). Where scroll-driven animations are supported it grows linearly to `100dvh` over the first header-height of page scroll; elsewhere it keeps the shorter height. Measured in Chrome at 1440×640 and 1440×800 at scroll 0, 32, 64 and further: the Partner sidebar's bottom stays at the window edge with Collapse 12px above it; Brand's shorter menu fits; at 1440×1400 there's no page scroll and no change. After this change: Vitest 252/252, format and typecheck pass; the same six browser specs pass 28/28 on Chromium desktop + mobile.

- **Sidebar width and content gutter (follow-up, 2026-11-25):** the user found the sidebar narrow and the gap to the content too large. Before/after mockups were rendered on the running app with injected CSS. The user chose **option B**: wider sidebar, constant gutter, content up to 1600px, left-aligned. Implemented with three tokens on `.wd-portal`:
  - `--wd-portal-sidebar` goes from 240 to 288px. The mockup used 280px, but the bold current item "Automation and approvals" still wrapped there; 287px is the measured need.
  - `--wd-portal-gutter` is 16px on phones, 24px from 600px and 32px from 1024px.
  - `--wd-portal-content-max` is 1600px.
  - **Containers on the shared tokens:** the `.wd-portal-page` class (Partner and Brand dashboards, Brand partner, Lead registrations, Brand sharing, Media kit), `.wd-portal .wd-main` (FoundationShell pages inside the portal), `.wd-home-main`, and Professional's `.pro-main`/`main` and `.pro-access` all use these tokens and align left. Inner 1280px wrappers (`.rec-page`, `.wd-crm`, Outreach and Onboarding pages) no longer re-centre.
  - **Measured before:** Home's gap was 40px at 1440 and 260px at 1920, and My leads' was 24px and 160px. Five labels wrapped.
  - **Measured after,** sweeping every sidebar destination (Partner 11, Brand 3, Professional 11) at 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920, expanded and collapsed (200 page checks): sidebar 288/72px, gap 32px ±2, no wrapped label (including the bold current item), no horizontal overflow, and content 1600px at 1920.
  - **Also fixed:** on short pages the white sidebar column stopped where the content ended. The portal body is now at least the window height below the header; the sidebar bottom equals the window height at 640, 900 and 1400px with no added scroll.
  - **Gates after this change:** Vitest 252/252, format, typecheck and build pass. Browser (Chromium desktop + mobile) 48/48: `portal-header`, `portal-home`, `partner-navigation`, `professional-ui`, `brand-lead-visibility`, `accessibility`, `portals`, `crm`, `outreach-templates`, `finance`, `automation`.

**Reused:** `PortalShell`, `AccountMenu`, `usePortalContext`, foundation tokens and focus styles. No backend or schema change.

**Fixed during inspection:** the first mobile layout wrapped Partner's account onto a third row (164px header) and, on Brand and Professional, let the labelled button overlap the experience chip at 390px and Brand's at 600px. The two-row phone layout and non-shrinking masthead above replace it. A later run of `professional-ui.spec.ts` found that at 320px, under that test's font loading, the Professional branding missed row 1 by a pixel. That pushed it onto row 2 and squeezed the avatar 15px past the edge. The explicit line break and the minimum button width fix this.

**Intentional reference differences:** the masthead keeps the experience chip (“Partner”, “Brand”, “Professional”) where v2 shows a workspace chip, so the workspace name appears once, at the account button. The v2 Help, Messages and Notifications buttons and its cross-resource placeholder remain out of scope; the field keeps “Search leads…”.
